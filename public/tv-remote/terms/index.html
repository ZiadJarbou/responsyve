What the subscription provides

An active trial or paid subscription provides continuing access to directional buttons, touchpad, supported keyboard input, voice, app shortcuts, volume and playback controls for compatible Android TV and Google TV devices. Availability depends on the TV, firmware and receiving app. Samsung Tizen and LG webOS are not supported. A subscription is required for remote features in this planned commercial release. Installation alone does not start a trial or billing.

Seven-day trial and monthly price

Eligible users can start a seven-day free trial by accepting the offer in Google Play checkout. Eligibility depends on the offer available to your Play account. Users who are not eligible see the applicable paid offer before confirming. A payment method may be required.

After the trial, the subscription automatically becomes paid and renews monthly unless canceled before the trial ends. The planned UAE price is AED 3.99 per month. Other countries use the local-currency price shown in Google Play. Checkout displays your actual price, billing period, trial eligibility, tax treatment and next charge date. No purchase can be made through this information page.

Payment and renewal

Google Play charges the selected payment method and manages renewals. The subscription continues monthly until canceled. Check your Play confirmation for billing dates. Price changes and any required notices or consent follow Google Play's rules and applicable law. Responsyve does not collect your card details.

Manage or cancel

Open Google Play subscriptions with the purchasing Google account. Select TV remote for Android, choose Cancel subscription and complete the confirmation. On Android, you can also open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. The app includes a Manage subscription link.

Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first monthly charge. Cancellation stops future renewal; access generally continues until the trial or paid period ends, subject to any immediate cancellation or refund option offered by Google Play or required by law. Uninstalling, clearing app storage or forgetting your TV does not cancel billing. See Google's cancellation instructions.

Restore and verify access

Use Restore purchases while signed into the Google Play account used to subscribe. Internet access is required to start, restore and periodically verify access. Offline access is limited and may require fresh verification after restarting your phone. Local TV control also requires a working connection to the TV. Pending or failed payments, expiry and revoked purchases may interrupt access. Restoring a subscription does not restore deleted TV pairings.

Eligibility depends on Google Play's refund policies and applicable consumer rights. Cancellation does not automatically refund earlier payments.
