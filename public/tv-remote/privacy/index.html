Scope

This policy covers the mobile app, subscription verification service and support communications. Google Play, YouTube, phone keyboard providers and TV manufacturers have their own policies for information they process.

TV discovery and saved connections

The app discovers compatible Android TV and Google TV devices on your local network. It processes TV names, network addresses, connection status and pairing information to connect to the TV you select. Saved TV names, addresses, certificate fingerprints, preferences and app shortcuts are stored privately on your phone. The private pairing key is held in Android Keystore. Saved connections are not sent to Responsyve's subscription server.

Commands, text and YouTube searches

Remote button commands, touchpad actions and supported typed text travel to the selected TV. Your chosen phone keyboard processes what you type under its own settings and policies. Choosing Search YouTube sends a results link containing your submitted query to the TV, and YouTube processes the search. Responsyve's verification service does not receive these commands, text or queries. Avoid entering passwords or other sensitive information into remote text fields.

Microphone

Voice input starts when you activate it and allow Android microphone permission. During a session, audio is streamed to the selected TV's voice service. The app does not save voice recordings or send them to Responsyve's subscription server. The TV's assistant may use online services under its own policies. You can revoke microphone permission in Android settings; other remote controls do not require it, subject to subscription access.

Subscription verification and payment

Google Play handles checkout and payment credentials. Responsyve does not receive card numbers or payment-account credentials. Customer subscription checkout is not yet available. When enabled, the app will send a Google Play purchase token, a random installation identifier and a one-time request value over HTTPS to our verification service. It checks subscription status and expiry with Google Play, acknowledges valid new purchases and returns a signed access grant. These checks provide subscription access and help prevent unauthorized use. Google processes purchases under Google's privacy policy.

Our public verification endpoint is hosted on a Hostinger VPS in Mumbai, India. The application verifier processes requests in memory and has no subscription or customer-profile database. A purchase-check helper is deployed on Google Cloud Run in europe-west1 (Belgium), with customer purchase verification disabled until Google Play setup and testing are complete. When enabled, our Hostinger verifier will send only the purchase token over HTTPS to this helper to check and acknowledge the subscription with Google Play. It will not forward the installation identifier, request nonce, reviewer code, TV connections, commands, typed text or audio. The helper uses an attached Google service identity and has no customer purchase database. The signed access grant is issued by our Hostinger verifier. The phone privately stores its installation identifier and access grant.

Reviewer authentication

Authorized reviewers may use a private reusable access code instead of purchasing a subscription. The app sends the code, installation identifier and one-time request value over HTTPS to our verification service. It compares a configured hash and returns a signed reviewer grant lasting up to ten minutes. The phone stores the code in private app storage, excluded from standard Android backup, to renew reviewer access. End reviewer access removes the stored code and grant without deleting saved TV connections or canceling a subscription. Reviewer authentication does not create a Responsyve user account or a Google Play purchase.

Google Play Billing SDK

The app includes Google Play Billing 9.1.0. Besides purchase operations, the bundled library contains a Google diagnostic-reporting path for billing events, results, failures and technical context. Its reporting transport can attach app/package version, Android version, device model/manufacturer/build information, network type, locale, time-zone offset and SIM operator information where available. Google also receives connection metadata when reporting connects to its services. This reporting is separate from our verification server. We do not receive a copy of Google's SDK diagnostic records and cannot directly erase them from Google's systems.

The app does not add advertising, a Responsyve analytics tracker or a separate crash-reporting service. This does not mean Google's Billing library is free of diagnostics. Google's own use, retention and deletion practices are described in Google's privacy policy and data retention explanation.

Support communications

When you email info@responsyve.co, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle billing or privacy enquiries. Our company mailbox uses Google Workspace. Do not send passwords, pairing codes, full card details or private keys. We retain support correspondence while it is needed to answer your request, address recurring issues, meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. You can request deletion at the same address. Google Workspace processes mailbox information under its service and privacy terms.

Sharing and tracking

Responsyve has not added advertising, a separate analytics tracker or a separate crash-reporting SDK. Google Play Billing diagnostics are described above. We do not sell personal information or use searches, commands or voice audio for advertising. Recipients include your selected TV and its services, Google Play, the verification hosting provider and our email provider as described above. Information may also be disclosed where legally required, to protect users and service security, or in a business transfer with applicable safeguards and notice.

Retention and security

Local saved connections remain until you forget a TV, clear app storage or uninstall. Forgetting a TV removes its connection record; it does not remove the app's Keystore identity, other preferences or records kept by the TV. Clearing app storage removes saved connections and preferences and requires pairing again. Standard Android backup of app data is disabled. Google and TV services retain their own records under their policies.

Paid access grants last at most 24 hours and may expire sooner with a subscription. Reviewer grants last at most ten minutes. Grants are periodically refreshed online. Local TV connections use encrypted transport; verification uses HTTPS. No system is completely secure. Our website is hosted on a Hostinger VPS in Mumbai, India. Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request times, requested paths and technical status information to deliver and secure the site. The verification service disables request access logs and avoids recording request bodies, review codes, purchase tokens, signed grants, typed text or audio. Website access and operational error logs may contain technical request metadata. Nginx access logs are rotated daily with 14 rotated files retained; this is a rotation policy, not an exact deletion deadline for every request or provider record. Server system journals are bounded by configured storage limits (currently 1 GB), rather than a fixed time-based retention period. Separate provider records are governed by their service terms and legal obligations. We do not promise immediate erasure of provider-controlled records. Technical records are used for operation, security and troubleshooting. We do not operate an advertising tracker on these app information pages.

Google Cloud Run automatically records platform request metadata even though our application access logs are disabled. This can include the caller IP address, request URL and method, time, status, user agent, sizes and latency. Normal verification traffic reaches the helper from our Hostinger service. Cloud Logging currently retains ordinary request, container and system logs in our project for 30 days and required audit logs for 400 days. These log buckets use Google's global location; the helper's Belgium region is not a promise that every provider record stays in Belgium. Our code avoids logging request bodies, purchase tokens and authentication secrets. Google may keep separate service, security or legally required records under its terms. We assess deletion requests for records under our control and seek provider assistance where applicable; we do not promise immediate removal from provider backups or required audit records.

Your choices and requests

You can manage microphone permission, forget saved TVs, clear app storage and manage your subscription in Google Play. The app does not create a separate Responsyve account. Uninstalling or removing local data does not cancel a subscription. Email info@responsyve.co to ask about access, correction, deletion, objection or other rights available under applicable law. We may request proportionate verification. Legal obligations or disputes may require retention of some records. Google and TV providers handle requests about their own data.

International processing and children

Responsyve operates in the UAE. Providers such as Google may process information in other countries. Our providers may process information outside your country. We use service agreements and applicable safeguards where required for international processing. This app is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. It is a remote utility, not a parental-control or content-filtering service. Independent TV apps, YouTube and voice assistants apply their own account, content and age settings. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact info@responsyve.co so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Requesting deletion

Email info@responsyve.co with the subject TV Remote data deletion. Describe whether your request concerns support correspondence, local app data or verification-related records. Use the same email address you used for support where possible. Do not send passwords, reviewer codes, TV pairing codes, purchase tokens, private keys or identity documents. We may ask for proportionate information needed to locate records and confirm the request, and respond under applicable law.

We remove eligible support records and copies under our control when the request is verified, unless retention is necessary for a legal obligation, security issue or dispute. Ordinary support correspondence follows the case-by-case retention policy described above; there is no fixed automatic support-mail deletion timer. Mail moved to Gmail Trash can remain recoverable for up to 30 days before its normal removal, and provider recovery, backup or legally held copies can follow different schedules. We do not promise immediate erasure from every provider system.

We do not maintain a customer purchase database to delete. Where a request concerns provider-controlled records, we seek appropriate provider assistance or explain how to contact that provider. Google purchase records and TV/YouTube/assistant records are controlled by those services. Local saved TVs can be forgotten in the app; Android Clear storage removes this app's local data and requires pairing again. End reviewer access removes the stored review credential and grant. Deleting data or uninstalling does not cancel Google Play subscriptions; manage cancellation separately in Google Play.
