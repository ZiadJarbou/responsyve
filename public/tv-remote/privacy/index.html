Scope

This policy covers the mobile app, subscription verification service and support communications. Google Play, YouTube, phone keyboard providers and TV manufacturers have their own policies for information they process.

TV discovery and saved connections

The app discovers compatible Android TV and Google TV devices on your local network. It processes TV names, network addresses, connection status and pairing information to connect to the TV you select. Saved TV names, addresses, certificate fingerprints, preferences and app shortcuts are stored privately on your phone. The private pairing key is held in Android Keystore. Saved connections are not sent to Responsyve's subscription server.

Commands, text and YouTube searches

Remote button commands, touchpad actions and supported typed text travel to the selected TV. Your chosen phone keyboard processes what you type under its own settings and policies. Choosing Search YouTube sends a results link containing your submitted query to the TV, and YouTube processes the search. Responsyve's verification service does not receive these commands, text or queries. Avoid entering passwords or other sensitive information into remote text fields.

Microphone

Voice input starts when you activate it and allow Android microphone permission. During a session, audio is streamed to the selected TV's voice service. The app does not save voice recordings or send them to Responsyve's subscription server. The TV's assistant may use online services under its own policies. You can revoke microphone permission in Android settings; other remote controls do not require it, subject to subscription access.

Subscription verification and payment

Google Play handles checkout and payment credentials. Responsyve does not receive card numbers or payment account credentials. To check subscription access, the app sends a Google Play purchase token, a random installation identifier and a one-time request value over HTTPS to our verification service. It checks subscription status and expiry with Google Play, acknowledges valid new purchases and returns a signed access grant. This provides subscription access and helps prevent unauthorized use. Google Play's processing is described in Google's privacy policy.

The prepared verification service processes requests in memory and has no subscription database. The phone privately stores its installation identifier and access grant. Hosting systems may process network metadata such as IP addresses when receiving requests. Production hosting provider, processing regions, operational metadata collected and log retention: [TO CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLICATION]. Purchase tokens, request bodies, typed text and audio must be excluded from operational logs.

Support communications

When you email info@responsyve.co, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle billing or privacy enquiries. Our company mailbox uses Google Workspace. Do not send passwords, pairing codes, full card details or private keys. Support correspondence retention and mailbox processing arrangements: [TO CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLICATION].

Sharing and tracking

The current app has no advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK added by Responsyve. We do not sell personal information or use searches, commands or voice audio for advertising. Recipients include your selected TV and its services, Google Play, the verification hosting provider and our email provider as described above. Information may also be disclosed where legally required, to protect users and service security, or in a business transfer with applicable safeguards and notice.

Retention and security

Local saved connections remain until you forget a TV, clear app storage or uninstall. Forgetting a TV removes its connection record; it does not remove the app's Keystore identity, other preferences or records kept by the TV. Clearing app storage removes saved connections and preferences and requires pairing again. Standard Android backup of app data is disabled. Google and TV services retain their own records under their policies.

Signed access grants last at most 24 hours, may expire sooner with a subscription and are periodically refreshed. Local TV connections use encrypted transport; verification uses HTTPS. No system is completely secure. Retention rules for infrastructure logs and support messages must be finalized before launch.

Your choices and requests

You can manage microphone permission, forget saved TVs, clear app storage and manage your subscription in Google Play. The app does not create a separate Responsyve account. Uninstalling or removing local data does not cancel a subscription. Email info@responsyve.co to ask about access, correction, deletion, objection or other rights available under applicable law. We may request proportionate verification. Legal obligations or disputes may require retention of some records. Google and TV providers handle requests about their own data.
