Support

Effective 17 October 2026

Help with TV remote for Android by Responsyve DWC-LLC. Contact info@responsyve.co for app, subscription and privacy enquiries. This is a pre-release page; the Play subscription release is not yet available. Support hours and a response-time commitment have not yet been set.

Connect your TV

  • Connect the phone and Android TV or Google TV to the same local network. Guest Wi-Fi or client isolation can prevent communication.
  • Turn on your TV and open the app. It attempts to reconnect to the last saved TV when available.
  • For a new TV, use the TV selection card, choose your device and enter the pairing code displayed on the TV when prompted. Automatic reconnection still requires initial pairing.
  • The supported setup requires no developer options, ADB debugging or TV companion. Samsung Tizen and LG webOS are not supported.

TV not found or connection lost

Check that the TV is awake and both devices are on the same network. Reopen the app and select the TV. A VPN or network isolation may prevent discovery. Restart the app, TV or router if necessary. If the TV was reset or its identity changed, forget that TV and pair again. Avoid clearing app data or uninstalling as a first step because it removes saved connections.

Arabic YouTube search

Choose Keyboard mode and use your usual phone keyboard to enter Arabic. Tap Search YouTube to open results on the connected TV. You do not need to open YouTube's letter grid first. The app uses the languages enabled in your phone keyboard. This action opens a results link; direct editing of YouTube's own grid is not guaranteed. If results do not open, check the connection and that the TV can open YouTube, then try a short query. No language button or TV companion is needed.

Voice input

Allow microphone access when Android asks. Tap the microphone and speak during the active session. Check that another phone app is not using the microphone and that the TV's voice service can access the internet. Recognition and supported languages depend on the TV's assistant. You can revoke microphone access in Android settings.

Keyboard and app shortcuts

Tap the keyboard text area to use your phone's current keyboard. TV apps differ in the input methods and languages they accept. Use Search YouTube for Arabic YouTube queries. App shortcut buttons open configured destinations on the TV; behavior depends on installed apps and supported links. The remote subscription does not include streaming-service subscriptions.

Trial and billing

The planned Play release offers eligible users seven days free, followed by a monthly subscription. The planned UAE price is AED 3.99/month; actual local prices and renewal dates are displayed in Google Play before confirmation. A trial starts only after accepting the subscription offer. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

Manage or cancel through Google Play subscriptions with the purchasing account. Uninstalling does not cancel billing. Read the Subscription terms linked on this site. For refunds, use Google Play refund help.

Paid but access is missing

Confirm Google Play is using the account that purchased the subscription. Connect the phone to the internet and choose Restore purchases. Check subscription status in Google Play; a pending payment is not a completed purchase. If access is still missing, email info@responsyve.co with the app version and a description. Do not buy again just to troubleshoot.

Contact support safely

Email info@responsyve.co with the phone model, Android version, app version, TV brand/model, TV software version if known, affected TV app and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful after removing personal information. We may request a Google Play order reference for billing investigations; do not post it publicly. Never send passwords, pairing codes, full card details or private keys. Normal supported setup does not require TV developer mode.

Privacy and local data

Forget a TV to remove its saved connection record. Android's Clear storage removes saved connections and preferences and requires pairing again; it does not remove records held by Google or your TV. The app has no separate Responsyve login. Send privacy requests to info@responsyve.co and read the Privacy policy linked on this site. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription.
